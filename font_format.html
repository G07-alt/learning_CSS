Maharashtra is a major state in western India, bordered by the Arabian Sea to the west and several neighboring states. It was officially formed on May 1, 1960, after the division of the bilingual Bombay State. The state capital is Mumbai, which also serves as the financial and entertainment capital of India. It spans an area of 307,713 square kilometers, making it the third-largest state in India by land area. With a population of over 128 million people, it ranks as the second- or third-most populous state in the country. The official and most widely spoken language is Marathi. Physically, the state is shaped by the Western Ghats mountain range and the vast Deccan Plateau. Its economy is the largest in India, driven by finance, technology, manufacturing, and agriculture. Culturally, it is famous for historical sites like the Ajanta and Ellora Caves, as well as vibrant festivals like Ganesh Chaturthi.

Maharashtra is a major state in the western peninsular region of India, officially formed on May 1, 1960. It is the second-most populous state in the country and the third-largest by land area, spanning a diverse geography that includes the Western Ghats (Sahyadri range), the fertile coastal strip of Konkan along the Arabian Sea, and a vast portion of the Deccan Plateau. Mumbai serves as the state capital and India's primary financial and commercial center, while Nagpur functions as the winter capital. The state has a rich history shaped by early Buddhist empires, the Maratha Empire under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, and British rule, leaving behind world-renowned heritage sites such as the Ajanta Caves and Ellora Caves. Today, Maharashtra is an economic powerhouse driven by finance, information technology, manufacturing, and agriculture, with Marathi as its official language
